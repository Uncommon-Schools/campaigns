# Project Brief — 30th Anniversary Landing Page
**Prepared by:** Coordinator | **Date:** April 20, 2026 | **Status:** Draft — pending Anthony review

> This document defines the goals, audience, scope, and success criteria for the project. All team work should trace back to this brief.

---

## What We're Building

A single-page landing page celebrating 30 years of excellence at Uncommon Schools. The page is the connective tissue for the entire anniversary initiative — it brings together media, milestones, stories, and campaigns in one curated experience.

---

## Goals

1. Celebrate the 30-year milestone in a way that reflects the quality and ambition of the Uncommon brand
2. Connect all anniversary-related pieces (campaigns, events, content) in one place
3. Take the user on a journey — informational, emotional, modern
4. Serve as a living asset that can be linked to from all anniversary communications

---

## Audience

*Confirmed July 22, 2026 from the campaign's "30 Years of Impact overview" (activities-by-audience plan):*

- **Primary:** General public / education professionals (brand awareness) and donors ("Support the Next 30 Years" — feeds the salon series and "The Next 30" campaign)
- **Secondary:** Families and students (30-years-of-proof supports SY27-28 enrollment), staff and alumni ("We Are Uncommon"), boards, elected officials, and press

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Single-page responsive landing page
- Media integration (video, photography, data visualization TBD)
- Connection to anniversary campaign assets
- Analytics and tracking
- Launch and post-launch monitoring

**Out of scope:**
- Full site redesign (separate project)
- E-commerce or donation processing

---

## Constraints

- Must align with Uncommon brand standards
- Must be WCAG 2.2 AA accessible
- Launch date: TBD *(Anthony to confirm — campaign is live, so sooner is better; Aug regional kickoffs and Sept enrollment materials are natural link-to moments)*
- **Standalone site** — hosted independently; this project never edits uncommonschools.org (see D-07)
- Platform/hosting: Static HTML + GSAP on Cloudflare Pages *(Dev recommendation, see dev/01)*
- Body copy is locked to Development's approved "30 Years of Impact" copy (see decision D-06)
