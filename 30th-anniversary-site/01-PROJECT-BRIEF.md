# Project Brief — 30th Anniversary Landing Page
**Prepared by:** Coordinator | **Date:** April 20, 2026 | **Status:** Draft — pending Anthony review

> This document defines the goals, audience, scope, and success criteria for the project. All team work should trace back to this brief.

---

## What We're Building

A single-page landing page celebrating 30 years of excellence at Uncommon Schools. The page is the connective tissue for the entire anniversary initiative — it brings together media, milestones, stories, and campaigns in one curated experience.

---

## Goals

1. Celebrate the 30-year milestone in a way that reflects the quality and ambition of the Uncommon brand
2. Connect all anniversary-related pieces (campaigns, events, content) in one place
3. Take the user on a journey — informational, emotional, modern
4. Serve as a living asset that can be linked to from all anniversary communications
