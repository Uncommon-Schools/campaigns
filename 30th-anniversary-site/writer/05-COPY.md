## Approved Page Copy (verbatim from Drive, July 2026)

**Eyebrow:** Uncommon Schools
**Headline:** 30 Years of Impact
**Subhead:** For three decades, Uncommon Schools has worked to change the trajectory of children's lives through joyful and rigorous schools. What began with 72 scholars has grown into a community of 53 schools serving more than 20,500 students each year — and a legacy still unfolding.

### What 30 Years Makes Possible
*[headline photo]*

Since our founding, Uncommon has been driven by a simple but powerful conviction: children can achieve at extraordinary levels when they are known, challenged, and deeply supported.

As we mark 30 years of impact, we celebrate the students, families, educators, alumni, and supporters who have made this journey possible.

*[photo]*

We opened our schools in response to a need. In 1997, only 6% of Black and Latino students were proficient in 4th grade reading. That need hasn't changed. Today, only 13% of Black and Latino students are proficient in 8th grade math.

The average low-income 8th grade student reads at a 4th grade level. Low-income students drop out of high school at 5 times the rate of their high-income peers.

For the past 30 years, our students have defied these odds.

Uncommon has built and refined an approach that delivers results for students who need it most. That foundation positions us not only to meet today's challenges, but to lead through them—ensuring that every student continues to have access to the opportunities they deserve.

### A Legacy You Can Count
*[Image carousel, each photo displays 2 metrics]*

- **72** founding scholars
- **20,500+** students served annually today
- **119,125** elementary and middle school state exams passed
- **47,110** AP exams taken
- **2,987** SPED students
- **1,389** MLL students
- **7,035** high school graduates
- **91%** family satisfaction
- **162** alumni returning to work at Uncommon
- **2.6 million** books sold since 2010
- **30,000+** teachers and leaders reached worldwide in over 65 countries
- **72%** of Uncommon Schools staff identify as a person of color
- Our average tenure for principals is **11 years** at Uncommon, 6 years in the role

Behind every number is a student, a family, a teacher, and a budding future.

### Academic Excellence, Year After Year

For 30 years, Uncommon students have shown what is possible when excellence and opportunity meet.

The broader national landscape remains deeply unequal. Too many students still attend schools that are overcrowded, under-resourced, or unable to provide the academic preparation and support they need to thrive. Uncommon's results show that different outcomes are possible — but they also show that excellence must be intentionally built and continually strengthened.

That is where philanthropy plays a critical role. Public funding provides a foundation, but donor investment helps sustain the elements that make the Uncommon model exceptional: rigorous academics, strong teacher and leader development, targeted student supports, college access and alumni success programming, and enrichment opportunities that help students develop confidence, purpose, and voice.

Together, these investments allow Uncommon to do more than operate high-performing schools. They help us create the conditions for students to excel — in the classroom, on the path to college, and in the lives they go on to lead.

**Across our regions, our schools outperform local districts, and in several cases, exceed state averages and the proficiency rates of non-economically disadvantaged students.** In Newark, New York City, Rochester, Camden, and Boston, students are achieving at extraordinary levels because they are known, challenged, and supported every step of the way.

Our high school students continue to raise the bar. In the Class of 2025, **53%** of Uncommon students passed at least one AP exam — ahead of national public schools and statewide public school averages in New Jersey, New York, and Massachusetts.

**As of Fall 2025, 25% (1,742 out of 7,053) of Uncommon alumni have been accepted to or enrolled at the nation's most selective colleges.**

### From One School to a Movement

What started with one school and a bold belief has become a network serving tens of thousands of scholars.

But growth at Uncommon has never been about scale for its own sake. It has always been about widening access to excellent schools and creating more pathways for children to achieve, lead, and thrive.

Over the last 30 years, Uncommon has grown while remaining anchored in the same mission: preparing students for college, leadership, and a life of possibility and economic freedom.

### Where Students and Families Thrive

At Uncommon, that sense of belonging is part of what makes long-term success possible. This year, family satisfaction reached **91%**, a five-point increase in just one year.

Students thrive when schools pair strong instruction with deep relationships, joyful learning, and adults who fully believe in them.

**[Quote block]**
*"I love that my little girl is truly seen, heard, validated, and poured into. She sees people who look like her in positions of authority, and most of her classmates look like her. Daily, I leave her knowing that she is safe, supported, and cared for."* — Quote from LDR Slide 116

### Impact: The Ripple Effect of 30 Years

Uncommon's impact lives in every graduate who walks across a stage prepared for what comes next. It lives in every family whose sense of possibility has expanded. It lives in every educator who has been developed, every school community strengthened, and every young person who now sees new futures for themselves.

*[SSD & GRAD PHOTO CAROUSEL]*

That ripple effect continues to grow. Since 2010, Uncommon teachers and leaders have helped sell **2.6 million** books, and Uncommon has reached **30,000+** teachers and leaders worldwide across more than **65 countries**.

### Building What's Next

We are proud of what the last 30 years have made possible: academic excellence, stronger communities, and thousands of young people stepping into futures filled with promise.

As we celebrate 30 years of impact, we remain focused on the work ahead: opening doors, expanding opportunity, and ensuring that even more children can learn, grow, and thrive. With continued support, we will grow the number of students we serve, invest an exceptional student experience, and invest in state-of-the-art schools in communities that need them most.

Support from our community makes it possible to:

*[Each bullet aligned with a corresponding photo]*

- **Expand College Access & Success (CAS) programming**, ensuring every student is supported from high school through college graduation and beyond
- **Strengthen academic programming**, including AP course access, tutoring, and intervention that drives student achievement
- **Invest in teacher and leader development**, equipping educators to deliver rigorous, joyful instruction in every classroom
- **Grow enrichment opportunities**, from arts and athletics to career exploration and real-world learning experiences
- **Sustain and expand high-quality schools**, bringing Uncommon's model to more students and communities

Every investment is a direct investment in students — in their growth, their confidence, and their future.
