# Content Strategy — 30th Anniversary Site
**Prepared by:** US-Writer | **Status:** 🟡 Draft v1 — July 22, 2026 (pending Anthony sign-off)

> Defines the narrative arc, section structure, and emotional journey of the landing page. Built on research in 01-RESEARCH.md. This is the foundation everything else builds on — approved before copy begins.

> **Context that shapes this strategy:** Development (Adna) has already written approved page copy — see [05-COPY.md](./05-COPY.md) — and the org-wide campaign launched externally July 1, 2026. Our job is no longer to invent the narrative from scratch; it is to **honor the approved copy while elevating it into a media-rich, curated journey** that the flat copy doc can't deliver on its own. Where this strategy goes beyond the approved copy (timeline, video embeds, story modules), those are additive layers, not rewrites.

## Narrative Arc

**Through-line: "From 72 scholars to a movement — and the road ahead."**

The page tells one story in three acts, anchored by the number that makes 30 years tangible:

1. **Act I — The Need and the Bet (1997).** A city in crisis, a bipartisan law, two founders, one floor of classrooms, 72 scholars. The stakes: in 1997 only 6% of Black and Latino students were proficient in 4th-grade reading.
2. **Act II — Proof at Scale (1997–2026).** The bet paid off, over and over: first class 100% college acceptance, network formation, Blue Ribbons, *Teach Like a Champion* reaching 65+ countries, 53 schools, 20,500+ students, alumni graduating college at 4x the national low-income rate. The numbers are the spine; the people are the heart ("30 for 30" stories).
3. **Act III — The Next 30.** The need hasn't disappeared (13% proficiency in 8th-grade math today). Uncommon is positioned to lead through it. The reader is invited in: support, enroll, share.

The arc deliberately starts *before* Uncommon exists (the need), so the founding reads as an answer — and ends *after* the celebration (the next 30), so the anniversary reads as momentum, not nostalgia.

## Primary CTA

**Primary: "Support the Next 30 Years"** — this is the CTA the approved (Development-written) copy builds toward, and the anniversary campaign's donor track ("The Next 30" fundraising campaign) depends on it.

**Secondary hierarchy** (persistent but subordinate — nav/footer/inline, never competing with the primary band):
1. **Enroll** — the campaign explicitly uses 30-years-of-proof to build family trust for SY27-28 enrollment (Sept materials)
2. **Watch the stories** — "30 for 30" video hub keeps visitors engaged and returning
3. **Share** — social amplification with campaign hashtag

*Rationale:* one page cannot ask everyone for everything. The narrative structure lets each audience self-select — donors reach the primary CTA through the impact case; families exit earlier via Enroll; staff/alumni/press engage through stories. (Note: donation *processing* is out of scope per the project brief — CTA links to existing giving page.)

## Voice Direction

- **Base:** the approved copy's voice is the page's voice — confident, evidence-forward, warm. Don't rewrite it; frame it.
- **Celebratory but not self-congratulatory.** The anniversary construct is "We Are Uncommon" — the celebration is of students, families, educators, alumni, and supporters, never the institution patting itself on the back. "We are celebrating you."
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim carries a number or a name. "Unequaled in our track record" is earned by 4x college graduation, not asserted.
- **Reflective → forward-looking.** Past tense opens sections; future tense closes them. The page's last word is about what's next, not what was.
- **Additive microcopy** (timeline entries, section transitions, video captions) follows brand voice: short, declarative, active. Headlines in Knockout caps ≤6 words where possible ("30 YEARS OF IMPACT", "A LEGACY YOU CAN COUNT").
- **Positioning care:** the 30 years are Newark-anchored. Regional content uses "Backed by 30 years…" framing per brand guidelines — the page should celebrate the network without implying every region is 30 years old.
