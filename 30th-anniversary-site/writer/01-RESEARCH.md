## Brand Parameters (for this page)

### Logo
- **Name:** "Contemporary Block" — "30 YEARS OF IMPACT" in layered blue + gold blocks
- **Color variant for landing page:** Standard (blue/gold) — default for all digital
- **Gold Glitter variant:** Available for high-impact/celebratory moments on the page (hero, milestone sections)
- **Rule:** Anniversary mark must always appear alongside the Uncommon Schools logo in an approved lockup — never alone

### Primary Colors
| Color | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Uncommon Navy | `#1034B4` | Primary — backgrounds, headlines, dominant color |
| Uncommon Yellow / Gold | `#FFB500` | Secondary — accents, logo, key stats, CTAs |
| Black | `#000000` | Body text, supporting elements |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, reversed text |

> **Accessibility note:** Uncommon Yellow is not accessible at small sizes. Only use for logo or text above 19pt. All body copy → Navy or Black.

### Secondary Colors (use sparingly, one or two max)
| Color | HEX |
|---|---|
| Maroon | `#A60056` |
| Orange | `#FF6800` |
| Green | `#00973A` |
| Purple | `#4500A4` |

Secondary colors are accent-only, never as background colors. If used, always in combination with primary palette.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines | Knockout 49 (Liteweight) | All caps for ≤6 words; title case for longer |
| Subheads / school names | Knockout 48 | Narrower, same family |
| Supporting headlines | Knockout 29 | Up to 3 paragraphs of body use |
| Body copy | Libre Franklin | Google Font — web-safe, free |
| Bold / italic body | Libre Franklin Bold / Italic | |

**Leading formulas:**
- Headlines: point size × 0.9
- Body: point size × 1.25

### Photography Style
- Documentary/photojournalistic — authentic, candid moments
- Students as the clear subject — in uniform, in real school environments
- Warm, joyful, diverse — not staged studio shots
- Blurred backgrounds, natural lighting preferred
- Close-up and medium shots
