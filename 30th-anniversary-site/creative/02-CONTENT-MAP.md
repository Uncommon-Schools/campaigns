## Section Goals

Per-section feel/understand/do is defined in the [Emotional Journey Map](../writer/02-CONTENT-STRATEGY.md#emotional-journey-map) — Creative should design to those beats: awe → moral clarity → momentum → conviction → respect → warmth → connection → agency.

## CTA Strategy

- **One primary:** "Support the Next 30 Years" — its own full-width band at page end, gold on navy.
- **Secondary, persistent but subordinate:** Enroll (sticky nav + inline after section 7), Watch the Stories (section 8), Share (footer + section 8). Never two competing CTAs in the same viewport.
