## How This Folder Works

Direction and content mapping come first — wireframing before the page sections are agreed locks in the wrong structure. Each file must be reviewed and approved before the next begins.

**AI design tools** (Midjourney, Figma AI, etc.) are used at the comp stage. All AI outputs go through a review step before anything is handed to Dev.
