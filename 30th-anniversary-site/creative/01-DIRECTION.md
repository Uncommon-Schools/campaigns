# 01 — Creative Direction
**30th Anniversary Landing Page | US-Creative**
**Status:** ✅ Draft — Pending Review

---

## Tone & Emotional Register

**Bold. Celebratory. Earned.**

This page marks 30 years of closing the college completion gap for kids who were told it couldn't be done. The tone is not nostalgic — it is proud and forward-looking. The user should feel the weight of what's been built, and leave energized about what comes next.

Three emotional beats the page should hit, in order:
1. **Pride** — look at what we've built together
2. **Proof** — here's what 30 years of impact actually looks like
3. **Momentum** — this is where we're headed next

What this page should NOT feel like: a generic nonprofit milestone page. No soft pastels, no vague "making a difference" language, no stock photography energy. This is Uncommon — specific, data-driven, bold.

---

## Visual Direction

**Primary color version of the "Contemporary Block" 30th anniversary lockup.** The navy + gold palette is the right call for a landing page — it's bold, it's the brand, and it signals institutional excellence without looking institutional.

The Gold Glitter variant may appear once — hero or closing section only — as a punctuation mark. It was designed for exactly this kind of milestone moment.

**Key design principles for this page:**
- The anniversary mark is always secondary to the Uncommon Schools logo — approved lockup only
- Navy and yellow dominate — black and white provide contrast and breathing room
- Secondary colors (Maroon, Orange, Green, Purple) are available sparingly for program-level or school-specific callouts only — never as backgrounds
- White background is the default for sections containing photography
- Typography is Knockout (headlines) + Libre Franklin (body) — no exceptions

---

## What to Use from the Existing Brand

- **Photography is the strongest asset** — authentic, emotionally resonant student and classroom imagery. Lead with it. Every section that can carry an image should.
- **The logo bars** (Uncommon's horizontal rule system) should be a recurring structural device — section dividers, headline underlines, and visual throughlines that connect sections as the user scrolls
- **Knockout 49** for all major headline moments — large, bold, all-caps for short lines (6 words or fewer); sentence case for longer statements
- **Uncommon Yellow** as the accent and energy color — used for the anniversary mark, key callout moments, and statistics. Not as background.
- **Uncommon Navy** as the dominant brand color — hero, section anchors, and anywhere the page needs weight

---

## What This Page Should NOT Feel Like

- A press release
- A PDF printed to web
- Generic nonprofit milestone content ("30 years of making dreams possible")
- Carousel-heavy or cluttered — every section has one job
- A different visual language from the Uncommon brand — this is not a standalone microsite identity
