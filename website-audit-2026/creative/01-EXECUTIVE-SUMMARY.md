# 01 — Executive Summary
**Uncommon Schools Website Audit | Phase 01: Dig**

> The top-line verdict, overall ratings, the 5 critical failures, and the full priority action list. Read this first.

---

## Verdict

The underlying mission is extraordinary. The site does not match it. What exists is a 2018-era institutional web presence — functional but visually flat, structurally incoherent, and built around the organization's internal logic rather than user needs.

**Overall Visual Rating: 5/10** — Does not feel like a top-tier organization. The brand has been refreshed; the site is not expressing it.

---

## 5 Critical Failures

1. **Brand misalignment** — Visual expression is generic nonprofit. Typography inconsistently applied. Color usage conservative to the point of invisibility.
2. **Homepage underperforms** — Rotating video hero delivers motion but almost no crawlable content, no audience gateway, no hierarchy.
3. **Enroll is a dead end** — Highest-priority CTA resolves to one sentence and a city picker. No steps, no trust-building, no Spanish.
4. **No design system** — Components look hand-assembled per page. Typography scale drifts page to page.
5. **Regional sites feel like different brands** — No unified design language across the network and its 5 regional subdomains.
