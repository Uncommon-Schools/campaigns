## Project Context

**Client:** Uncommon Schools — 20,000+ students, 52 schools, 30-year mission of getting kids to and through college.

**The brief:** Rebuild the website in-house. Mobile-first, visually modern, WCAG 2.2 AA compliant. Serves three audiences: students/families, staff/educators, donors.

**Brand note:** The brand has been recently refreshed. This audit assesses and elevates that identity in a web context — not a redesign from scratch.

**Quality bar:** Lippincott-level depth.
