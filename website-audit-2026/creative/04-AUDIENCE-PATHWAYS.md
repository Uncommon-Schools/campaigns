# 04 — Audience Pathway Analysis
**Uncommon Schools Website Audit | Phase 01: Dig**

> For each of the three core audiences, this file maps the ideal journey against the current broken reality. Friction points feed directly into Phase 02 persona and journey map work.

---

## Audience 1: Families / Students — "Enroll my child"

### Ideal Path
Homepage → understand what Uncommon is → find a nearby school → learn enrollment process → apply

### Current Reality
Video homepage → city picker → regional subdomain (different visual design, different structure) → school finder → individual school page (~200 words, dated 2018)

### Critical Failures
- No Spanish at any point in the flow — a significant portion of the target population is Spanish-speaking
- No comparative information between campuses — a parent choosing between two Brooklyn schools has nothing to compare
- Enrollment flow requires knowing your city/region *before* receiving any orientation about the network
- School pages are thin, dated, and undifferentiated

**Friction Rating: HIGH**

---

## Audience 2: Staff / Educators — "Join Uncommon or access PD"

### Ideal Path
Understand the culture → see growth investment → browse open roles → apply

### Current Reality
`/careers/` — two paragraphs of generic mission language, a jobs link, and a "Why Uncommon?" link that leads to the same two paragraphs

### Critical Failures
- No "Why Uncommon?" content that actually builds conviction
- The teacher story video on the homepage is compelling but buried in a carousel with no forward pathway
- No connection between Uncommon Sense, Teach Like a Champion, and the careers pipeline — the intellectual brand is completely disconnected from recruitment

**Friction Rating: HIGH**

---

## Audience 3: Donors — "Give with confidence"

### Ideal Path
See impact → understand where money goes → find the right giving level → donate

### Current Reality
Donate page has solid tiered copy ($100/$250/$1K/$5K with specific outcomes). FundraiseUp form is present. Alumni Impact page — the primary evidence for donor storytelling — **404s**. No annual report. No major donor pathway. No named giving section.

### Critical Failures
- Alumni Impact page is dead — donors who click the primary proof point hit a 404
- No major donor pathway or named giving section
- No annual report or financial transparency layer
- FundraiseUp form is below the fold on the donate page

**Friction Rating: MEDIUM-HIGH**
