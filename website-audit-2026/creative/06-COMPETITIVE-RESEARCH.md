# 06 — Competitive & Benchmark Research
**Uncommon Schools Website Audit | Phase 01: Dig**

> Five best-in-class site audits — within and outside the education sector — with specific callouts and what to adapt. Closes with 9 design patterns that should shape Phase 02.

---

## KIPP — kipp.org
*Primary competitor | 279 schools / 125K students*

**Strengths:** Audience testimonials (parent, teacher, alumna, director) on homepage. Emotional storytelling through direct quotes. Clear scale stat in hero.

**Weaknesses:** Homepage is JavaScript-dependent with minimal crawlable text — poor SEO and AI indexability. No performance claim comparable to Uncommon's CREDO distinction. No Uncommon Sense / Teach Like a Champion equivalent.

**Key insight:** Uncommon's CREDO claim — *"greatest academic gains of any large charter network in the country"* — is a verifiable superlative KIPP cannot match. On Uncommon's current site, it appears once, in body text, on `/about-us/`. **KIPP does not have this data. Uncommon is not using it.**

---

## Khan Academy — khanacademy.org

**What they do right:** Homepage value prop in under 10 words. Navigation is learner-first, not org-first. Content library structured for AI extraction — clear Q&A, structured answers, topic ownership.

**Adapt:** HS Curriculum Hub + Uncommon Sense should be topic-first content libraries, not reverse-chronological blogs.

---

## College Board — collegeboard.org

**What they do right:** Hard audience segmentation at the top level — Students | Educators | Higher Ed | Counselors. FAQ with JSON-LD schema on data-heavy pages. AI-extractable structure throughout.

**Adapt:** Audience gateway model on the homepage. Structured data on every results and data page.

---

## Year Up — yearup.org

**What they do right:** Student stories as full narrative arcs — before/during/after with specific outcomes. Primary action (Apply) dominant on every page. Impact quantified at multiple scroll depths.

**Adapt:** Every Uncommon graduate profile should follow this arc. The individual student story + CREDO stat combination is the most powerful content asset the site could have. **It does not currently exist.**

---

## University of Michigan — umich.edu

**What they do right:** Donor ecosystem with real depth — named giving, endowment, alumni giving, impact tiers, annual report all surface clearly. Thought leadership and donor pipeline are integrated.

**Adapt:** Uncommon's donor section needs this architecture. Impact report, major donor pathway, named giving, alumni giving — none exist currently.

---

## 9 Design Patterns to Adopt

1. **Audience gateway on homepage** — force a choice: Families | Educators | Donors. Route users immediately.
2. **CREDO performance claim as primary hero statement** — own it at every entry point, not once in body text
3. **Full-arc student stories** — before/during/after with specific outcomes, not one-line pull quotes
4. **Topic-first content library architecture** for HS Curriculum Hub + Uncommon Sense
5. **Structured data + FAQ schema** on all data-heavy pages (results, about, FAQ)
6. **Differentiated school profiles** — principal, campus facts, open enrollment dates, student demographics, comparison tools
7. **Donor ecosystem depth** — annual report, major donor pathway, named giving, alumni giving tier
8. **Educator pipeline connection** — Uncommon Sense → Teach Like a Champion → "Work with us" as a deliberate journey
9. **Spanish-language enrollment pathway** — minimum bilingual coverage on `/enroll/` and all school pages
