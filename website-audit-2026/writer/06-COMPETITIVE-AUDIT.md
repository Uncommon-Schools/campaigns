## KIPP (Primary Peer)

**Scale:** 279 schools, 210,000+ students, 52 regions nationally | kipp.org

### What They Do Well
- Audience-segmented content exists — not perfect, but present
- Regional sites have more depth than Uncommon's regional presence
- Alumni testimonials surface on homepage: rotating quotes from alumni, teachers, parents
- Clear brand hierarchy between KIPP Foundation and regional networks
- Larger content footprint → more external links → stronger AI search domain authority

### Where They're Weaker
- `/schools/` redirects to homepage — same broken IA problem Uncommon has
- College placement data less prominently featured than Uncommon's 96% stat
- Regional content quality is uneven — strong in NJ, thin in smaller markets
- "KIPP approach" page is undifferentiated — "belief that college prep education sets students up for success" describes every charter network in the country

### Key Insight
Uncommon has a performance claim KIPP cannot match: **"Highest-performing large charter network in the country" (Stanford CREDO, 2023).** This claim should be on the homepage, the Results page, the About page, every school page, and in the FAQ. It's currently treated as a footnote.

## Outside-Sector Exemplars

### Khan Academy — Education, Mission-Driven
- Homepage value prop in 10 words: *"Free world-class education for anyone, anywhere."*
- Navigation is product-first (what do you want to learn?) not org-first (who are we?)
- Content library is indexed, searchable, structured for AI extraction
- **Adapt:** Uncommon's HS Curriculum Hub + Uncommon Sense should be organized like a content library, not a blog archive

### College Board — College Prep
- Hard audience segmentation from first click: Students | K–12 Educators | Higher Ed | Counselors
- Data pages (SAT stats, AP results) structured for AI search and featured snippets
- **Adapt:** Adopt audience gateway nav; structure Results and data pages for AI extraction

### Year Up — Workforce, Mission-Driven Nonprofit
- Clean mission-driven homepage with a single primary action (Apply)
- Student stories as full arcs: before → program → after. Not one-line pull quotes.
- Impact numbers prominent and specific
- **Adapt:** Uncommon's alumni and impact stories need this full-arc treatment — a mother's story from lottery application to her child's college acceptance

### University of Michigan — Higher Ed
- Faculty research = thought leadership, prominently featured
- Alumni engagement built into the content architecture, not bolted on
- Donor ecosystem is deep: impact reports, named funds, scholarship stories
- **Adapt:** Model Uncommon's donor section on a university-style impact ecosystem — tiered giving, named opportunities, annual impact report

## 8 Content Patterns to Adopt

1. **Audience gateway on homepage** (College Board model) — force a choice from the first screen: Families | Educators | Donors. Every path from that point is audience-specific.

2. **Full-arc student stories** (Year Up model) — not "Tasha, 10th grade, loves learning." Full story: where she started, what changed, where she's going. This is what moves donors and convinces families.

3. **Answer-first content structure** (Khan Academy model) — lead with the direct answer, support with detail. Structure every substantive page for AI extraction.

4. **Lead with the CREDO performance claim** — "Highest-performing large charter network in the country" is the most powerful statement on the site. It's buried. Put it everywhere.

5. **Unified educator content hub** — consolidate Uncommon Sense + Books + HS Curriculum Hub + district partnerships under a single "For Educators" section with its own nav pathway. This is how Uncommon builds authority as a professional learning destination, not just a school network.

6. **Impact data as storytelling** — "$1,000 sponsors a student's arts education for a semester" is a start. Build a full impact page with year-specific outcomes, giving levels tied to specific student stories, and a running total of what donor contributions have funded.

7. **School finder with real content** — families should search by zip code and land on a real school profile: photos, a specific differentiator, a principal name, and an enroll CTA. Not a 200-word placeholder from 2018.

8. **Cross-link thought leadership to recruitment** — every Uncommon Sense post and every book page should have a "Work with us" module. The educators who love Teach Like a Champion are exactly the educators Uncommon wants to hire.
