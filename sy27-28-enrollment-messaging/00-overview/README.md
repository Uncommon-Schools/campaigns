## Working approach (decided 2026-06-08)
- **80/20:** 80% consistent national framework, 20% regional adjustment
- **Headlines:** universal, work across all regions
- **Supporting copy / RTBs:** localized using slide 5 of the deck as the proof bank
- **Data points** (acceptance %, scholarship $, years of impact): support and explain the chosen language; they are RTB material, not headline copy
- **Audience lens priority:** parents lead, headlines must not alienate teachers, donors, or board
- **Sequence:** headlines first, then regional RTBs and copy variations

## Core brand message
> **Greatness is already in them. We provide the runway.**

## Strategic frame
- **Enemy:** Zip code destiny
- **Hero:** Outstanding urban public schools
- **Functional:** Education engineered for college rigor
- **Economic:** Closing the opportunity gap, securing the future
- **Emotional:** Greatness is already in them, we provide the runway

## Four supporting pillars (network-wide)
1. **Academics & Results** — graduate college at 4x peer rate, AP pass rate 2x national average
2. **Programs & Facilities** — HS Student Talent and Voice, career-focused extracurriculars
3. **Student Supports & Services** — open to all (SPED, MLL), free Pre-K, uniforms, materials; 529 College Savings (NYC, NJ, TBC)
4. **Track record** — 30 years of impact

## Funnel and messaging strategy

| Stage | Strategy | Channels | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Bold headlines that spark curiosity in 3-5 sec | OOH, web, collateral, social | Impressions, search volume, site traffic, QR scans |
| Consideration | Region-customized with data, local + network proof, school benefits | Digital, email, social, web, collateral | Leads, follows, QR scans |
| Conversion | Direct CTAs: sign up, apply, tour | Digital, web, collateral | Applications |
