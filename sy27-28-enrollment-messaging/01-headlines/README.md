# Headlines — Working Draft v1

## Brief
- **Brand promise:** Greatness is already in them. We provide the runway.
- **Strategic frame:** Enemy = zip code destiny. Hero = outstanding urban public schools. Lenses = functional (college rigor) / economic (opportunity gap) / emotional (greatness is in them).
- **Rule:** Universal headlines only. Regional differentiation lives in RTBs and supporting copy (slide 5 of source deck).
- **Audience lens:** parents lead. Headlines must not alienate teachers, donors, or board.
- **Data points are RTBs, not headlines.** No "95%" or "$13M" in headlines.
- **What done looks like:** a shortlist Anthony reacts to. Cuts and refines. Top 3-5 per funnel stage go to Thursday review.

## How to read this doc
For each candidate:
- **Stage** — Awareness / Consideration / Conversion
- **Leans on** — pillar(s) and lens(es) it activates
- **Regional stress test** — short note on whether the headline holds with each region's slide 5 RTBs
- **Notes** — flags, risks, evolutions

## Awareness — bold, broad, 3-5 second hook

### A1. Greatness lives here.
- **Leans on:** brand promise (compressed), emotional lens
- **Stress test:** Holds across all 5 regions. RTB layer carries proof.
- **Notes:** Most direct compression of the promise. Risk: too soft without RTB. Pair with strongest regional data.

### A2. We don't shape greatness. We give it room to run.
- **Leans on:** brand promise (long form), emotional + functional
- **Stress test:** Holds in all regions.
- **Notes:** Closer to the promise's verbatim. Strong for OOH where the line can breathe.

### A3. Their future does not belong to a zip code.
- **Leans on:** enemy frame, economic lens
- **Stress test:** Strongest in NYC, Newark, Camden. Still true in Boston, Rochester.
- **Notes:** Names the enemy without lecturing. Parents recognize this fear instantly.

### A4. Built for the long run, not the test next week.
- **Leans on:** track record + academics, functional + economic
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere. "Long run" implicitly carries 30/20 years.
- **Notes:** Differentiates from test-prep mills and short-term wins.

### A5. The runway starts here.
- **Leans on:** brand promise (metaphor only), emotional
- **Stress test:** Holds, but most evocative when paired with a flight/future visual.
- **Notes:** Visual-dependent. Strong for video and OOH; weaker for text-only ads.

### A6. Find your voice. Discover your purpose.
- **Leans on:** existing core, emotional
- **Stress test:** Already in market, proven across regions.
- **Notes:** Keep as a continuity anchor. Don't retire; rotate alongside new lines.

### A7. Potential meets preparation.
- **Leans on:** academics + emotional
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere.
- **Notes:** Evolution of "potential meets purpose" from the meeting. "Preparation" reads more concrete and academic than "purpose" and ties to the college-prep track record.

### A8. Belief, backed by results.
- **Leans on:** emotional + academics + track record
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere.
- **Notes:** Evolution of "belief without boundaries." "Backed by results" cues RTB without naming a number.

### A9. Where college is the plan, not the surprise.
- **Leans on:** academics, functional + economic
- **Stress test:** Holds across all regions. RTBs vary by acceptance %.
- **Notes:** Reframes college as expectation, which is the org's actual delivery.

### A10. Free. Public. Outstanding.
- **Leans on:** access + academics
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere. Cuts through any "charter = private" confusion.
- **Notes:** Clean, three-word OOH workhorse. Pairs with any RTB.

## Consideration — turn curiosity into interest, room for proof

### C1. Thirty years of getting students to and through college.
- **Leans on:** track record + academics, functional
- **Stress test:** Holds for Boston (28), Camden, Newark, NYC (30). **Breaks for Rochester (20).** Needs Rochester variant: "Twenty years of getting students to and through college."
- **Notes:** First headline that requires a region-specific number swap, but the structure is universal.

### C2. The same belief in every classroom. The proof in every region.
- **Leans on:** track record + 80/20 logic, emotional + functional
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere. RTBs do the regional lift.
- **Notes:** Surfaces the network advantage without naming regions.

### C3. Tuition-free. World-class. Wide open.
- **Leans on:** access + academics + student supports
- **Stress test:** Holds in all regions.
- **Notes:** "Wide open" cues SPED, MLL, open enrollment. Use when supports are the regional differentiator (e.g., Rochester, NYC Pre-K).

### C4. They are ready. We are ready for them.
- **Leans on:** emotional + student supports
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere.
- **Notes:** Reciprocity. Strong for parent letters, email, brochures.

### C5. From Pre-K to college, every step is paid for, planned for, and ready.
- **Leans on:** supports + track record + access
- **Stress test:** Strongest in regions with Pre-K (Camden, NYC). For Newark, Rochester, Boston, swap "Pre-K" for K or relevant entry grade.
- **Notes:** Concrete promise. Risk: too long for OOH; live this in digital and brochures.

### C6. Sports, arts, AP, internships. All in. No cost.
- **Leans on:** programs + supports + access
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere. Regional RTBs name the specifics (Camden golf, NYC robotics, etc.).
- **Notes:** Hits the "free, world-class co-curriculars" pillar head-on.

### C7. The school families have trusted for three decades.
- **Leans on:** track record, emotional + functional
- **Stress test:** Holds for Boston (28), Camden, Newark, NYC. Rochester needs "two decades" variant.
- **Notes:** "Families trusted" beats "schools ranked." Parents lens.

### C8. Greatness, with the receipts.
- **Leans on:** brand promise + track record + academics
- **Stress test:** Holds everywhere.
- **Notes:** Modern, parent-vernacular. Receipts = data RTBs. Risk: tonally informal for some board/donor reads; keep for digital/social.

### CV3. Enrollment is open. Greatness is ready.

- **Leans on:** academics + functional + economic
