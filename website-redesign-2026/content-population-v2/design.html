Rules

  • Comment, do not edit. Comments go on the draft. If a fact in the facts table is wrong, comment on it there. Nobody resolves a comment until the page is final; Uncommon resolves them after that.
  • Final say decides. Uncommon names the person with final say and the reviewers for each page before its direction is due. Where reviewers disagree, final say decides. Final say also judges whether a draft follows the confirmed direction; one that does not goes back to Lindsey at no charge, and that is not a round: final say says so in a comment and sets the status back to the draft he was writing.
  • Lindsey works from the replies. Uncommon answers every comment on a draft with what Lindsey should do, or "No change". Lindsey changes nothing until the status says the page is waiting on him.
  • Changes cost money. Changing a direction after it is confirmed, or changing a page after it is final, is paid work: $100 an hour from the 50 pre-authorized hours, approved in advance by , and recorded by Uncommon in the Paid changes table of the entry. Comments in a round are not changes.
  • Eleven pages get a second round of comments: Homepage; Find a School; Enroll Now; About; Mission; Why Uncommon; Our Results; Student Experience; Careers; Working at Uncommon; Support Uncommon. Every other page goes from Draft 2 to final say marking it final.

Schedule for the main site pages

Business days. Uncommon observes a half day on November 25, closure on November 26 and 27, and closure from a half day on December 18 through January 1. A window that spans the closure pauses and resumes January 4. A late direction or late comments from Uncommon move that batch's dates by the same number of days.
