Rules

  • Comment, do not edit. Comments go on the draft box. If a fact in the facts table is wrong, comment on that too. Nobody resolves a comment until the page is Final. Lindsey changes nothing until the status says Comments ready.
  • Figures. The data reviewer, , checks every figure in the same three comment days.
  • A draft that does not follow the confirmed direction goes back to Lindsey to rewrite at no charge. That is not a round.
  • Changes cost money. After a direction is confirmed or a page is Final, a change is paid work: $100 an hour from the 50 pre-authorized hours, approved in advance by , and recorded under Changes after final in the entry.
  • The 26 pages with a second round. Main site: Homepage; Find a School; Enroll Now; About; Mission; Why Uncommon; Our Results; Student Experience; Careers; Working at Uncommon; Support Uncommon. Each regional site: the region home page; About the region; Enrollment. Every other page gets step 5 straight after Draft 2.
  • Regional pages are written once, for New York City, and adapted for the other four regions in the same window.

The schedule

Business days. Uncommon observes a half day on November 25, closure on November 26 and 27, and closure from a half day on December 18 through January 1. A window that spans the closure pauses and resumes January 4. A late direction or late comments from Uncommon move that batch's dates by the same number of days.
