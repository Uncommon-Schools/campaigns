**Verdicts:** CONFIRMED = matches the March audit claim. CHANGED = live site differs; current value given. NOT REPRODUCIBLE = could not observe the claimed thing; method noted.

**Headline:** the six main domains were migrated off WP Engine + Cloudflare onto a single **WordPress multisite** behind nginx at 134.209.175.171 sometime after March 2026.
