# 07 — Design Direction & Phase 02 Scope
**Uncommon Schools Website Audit | Phase 01: Dig**

> Forward-looking recommendations based on the audit. Covers visual tone, what to keep/elevate/rethink, audience archetypes, and the full scope of Phase 02 deliverables. This is where the build begins.

---

## What to Keep

**The photography.** Authentic, emotionally resonant, racially representative. It's the site's strongest visual asset. Keep and elevate — it's currently underused. Lead with it.

---

## What to Elevate

**The mission language and performance data.**

- *"Close the college completion gap"* — precise and powerful
- *"Greatest academic gains of any large charter network"* — a verifiable superlative no competitor can match

These phrases belong at the top of every visual hierarchy, not buried in body copy. The individual student story + CREDO stat combination is the most powerful content asset the site could have. It does not currently exist.

---

## What to Completely Rethink

| Element | Current State | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Rotating video carousel, no gateway | Audience gateway + CREDO proof in first viewport |
| Enroll experience | 1 sentence and a city picker | Dedicated product: steps, FAQ, Spanish, trust signals |
| Navigation | Org-chart taxonomy | Audience-first: Families / Educators / Donors |
| School pages | ~200 words, dated 2018 | Differentiated: principal, dates, demographics, compare |
| Donor section | Donate page only, Alumni Impact 404s | Full ecosystem: impact report, named giving, giving tiers |

---

## Visual Tone

**Bold without aggression. Warm without softness. Academic but not institutional.**

The refreshed brand supports this — the site is not currently expressing it. The new design must make the photography, mission, and verified data impossible to miss.

---

## Layout Direction

- Grid-first, whitespace-generous
- Let photography breathe — each module has one job
- Large, confident display type for performance claims and mission statements
- Body optimized for readability at all sizes
- Scale should feel editorial, not corporate

---

## Imagery Direction

- In-context, unposed, specific — students doing the work: reading, debating, presenting
- Staff in their environments
- No generic or stock-adjacent photography
- Unified photography direction applied across all regional subdomains

---

## Motion & Interaction

- Purposeful and restrained
- Scroll-triggered reveals on key statistics
- No autoplaying video with audio
- Hero: high-quality still image with secondary video option
- Carousel replaced with intentional storytelling structure

---

## Phase 02 Navigation Concepts (2 to develop)

1. **Audience-first gateway** — homepage leads with audience selector; each path has tailored content and CTAs
2. **Outcome-first hero** — CREDO data is the first thing you see; everything else flows from proof

---

## Audience Archetypes (Phase 02 Input)

| Archetype | Need | Biggest Friction Today |
|---|---|---|
| **The Searching Parent** | A school for their child — needs trust signals and a clear enrollment path | Empty enroll page, no Spanish |
| **The Mission Donor** | ROI on social impact — needs data, stories, financial transparency | Alumni Impact 404s, no giving ecosystem |
| **The Educator** | Career opportunity — needs culture, values, school-level info | No conviction-building content, careers page is empty |

---

## Phase 02 Deliverables (Pending Anthony + Coordinator Sign-Off)

**Proposed timeline:** April 14 – May 23, 2026

- 2 navigation concepts (audience-first vs. outcome-first)
- Wireframes: up to 10 page templates, 25 modules
- UX interaction flows (enrollment, donation, school finder)
- 3 audience archetypes
- Design system foundation: color tokens, type scale, component library
