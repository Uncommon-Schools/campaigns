# 02 — Visual Design Audit
**Uncommon Schools Website Audit | Phase 01: Dig**

> Assessment of the current visual expression of uncommonschools.org against the refreshed brand. Covers typography, color, imagery, component consistency, and regional brand cohesion.

---

## Typography

The site uses a sans-serif system with inconsistent sizing across pages. H1s in the homepage hero vary in weight and size across rotating slides — visual noise, not hierarchy. Body text is likely undersized on mobile with insufficient line-height.

The `/results/` page presents the 96% college acceptance stat — the single most powerful claim in the Uncommon story — as an unstyled H5 beneath an H3. The CREDO claim ("greatest academic gains of any large charter network in the country") appears once, in body paragraph text, on `/about-us/`. Neither receives design weight matching its importance.

**Rating: 6/10**

---

## Color Usage

Color is applied generically: white backgrounds, black text, minimal accent. The donate CTA is a standard blue link. The homepage hero uses a dark overlay on video rather than branded color fields. The site reads as colorless. Whatever boldness exists in the refreshed brand palette is absent from the current digital expression.

**Rating: 6/10**

---

## Imagery

The strongest visual asset on the site. Student and classroom photography is authentic, emotionally resonant, and racially representative. However:

- Images appear compressed and underperforming at display size
- Homepage hero video autoplays — impactful on fast connections, but a liability on mobile and for accessibility
- Regional sites use different photo libraries with inconsistent treatment — NYC reads warmer than the network site
- No unified photography direction applied across domains

**Rating: 8/10**

---

## Component Consistency

Components look hand-assembled per page. No consistent module logic is visible. Typography scale drifts page to page. No evidence of a documented design system or component library being applied across the site or its regional subdomains.

**Rating: 4/10**

---

## Brand Cohesion Across Regions

nyc.uncommonschools.org has a distinct visual character from the network site. Regional subdomains (Boston, Camden, Newark, NYC, Rochester) feel like separate brands — inconsistent visual language, inconsistent component use, different photo libraries. No unified design language connects the network to the regions.

**Rating: 3/10**

---

## Summary Ratings

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---|
| Typography | 6/10 |
| Color system | 6/10 |
| Photography | 8/10 |
| Component consistency | 4/10 |
| Brand cohesion across regions | 3/10 |
| **Overall** | **5/10** |
