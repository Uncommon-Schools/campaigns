## 1. Page 1. "How the work moves" and "How to review" are replaced by this, in the same place. The "Applies to every page" lines come out.

> **How the work moves**
>
> The pages are written in six batches between November 9 and January 28; the schedule is below. Each page's status line, in its facts table, says where the page is and whose turn it is. Whoever completes a step changes the status and adds the date.
>
> 1. **Direction.** Before a batch's direction window opens, Lindsey writes one paragraph under "Page direction" in each page's entry: what the page is for, who it is for, and its key messages. He sets the status to "Direction proposed". Within the two-day window the person with final say either sets the status to "Direction confirmed" or writes what should change under "Changes asked for"; Lindsey revises the paragraph and the person with final say confirms it. Lindsey writes to the confirmed direction.
> 2. **Draft 1.** Lindsey writes the page under "Draft 1" and sets the status to "Draft 1 delivered" on the last day of the batch's writing window. [Name] emails the page's reviewers that day.
> 3. **Comments.** Reviewers have three business days to comment on Draft 1. [Name] then has two business days to turn the comments into one set: replying under each comment with what Lindsey should do, closing the ones that will not be acted on, and getting the person with final say to decide where reviewers disagree. [Name] then sets the status to "Comments ready". Lindsey changes nothing before that.
> 4. **Draft 2.** Within five business days Lindsey writes the revised page under "Draft 2", replies "Done" under each comment he has acted on, and sets the status to "Draft 2 delivered". Draft 1 stays as it was. [Name] emails the person with final say.
> 5. **Final.** Within two business days the person with final say reads Draft 2 and sets the status to "Final", or comments on errors for Lindsey to fix in Draft 2 and then sets it to "Final". Only errors are fixed at this step. The last draft in the entry is the copy Spinutech loads.
>
> **The twenty-six pages with a second round** (listed under the schedule) skip step 5 after Draft 2. Instead, reviewers have three business days to comment on Draft 2, [Name] has two days to make one set and sets "Second-round comments ready", Lindsey writes "Draft 3" within five business days and sets "Draft 3 delivered", and the person with final say sets "Final".
>
> **Two more rules.** A draft that does not follow the confirmed direction goes back to Lindsey to rewrite at no cost, and that is not a round. After a page is final, or after a direction is confirmed, any change costs money: it comes out of the 50 pre-authorized hours at $100 an hour, needs [Name]'s approval first, and is recorded under "Changes after final" at the end of the entry.
>
> **How to review**
>
> Comment on the draft; do not edit it. If a fact in the facts table is wrong, comment on that too. Figures are checked by [Name], the data reviewer, in the same three days as everyone else's comments. If you think the direction is wrong, comment on the "Page direction" block, not on the draft; the person with final say decides whether it is a change. If Lindsey is missing information, he comments on "Source material". When reviewers disagree, the person with final say decides.
>

> | Direction proposed | The person with final say, two business days. |
> | Direction confirmed | Lindsey, writing. |

> | Comments ready | Lindsey, five business days. |
