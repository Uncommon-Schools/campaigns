## 01 Header (picture: 01-header.webp)
- The header is slightly taller, giving the logo and the navigation more room.
- The Uncommon Schools logo is larger for a stronger brand presence at the top of the page.
- The header's bottom corners are rounded so it sits as a shape over the hero image.

## 02 Feature cards (picture: 02-feature-card.webp)
- Feature cards like the "Find a School" card should be yellow (background), white selector and black button.
- The yellow card colour is preferred over blue because it adds more vibrancy and energy where we want the eye to go.

## 03 CTA buttons (pictures: 03a Enroll Now + menu, 03b Watch Video, 03c outlined arrow circle, 03d white/black arrow circles on yellow, 03e Learn More)
- CTA buttons are always in pill shape.
- Secondary CTA buttons are circles.

## 04 Section headlines and supporting text (pictures: 04a light background "Your connection", 04b dark background "Why families")
- Section headlines should be Knockout - 49 Liteweight.
- Section supporting text should be Libre Franklin - Medium.
- The headline has two colour combinations, white+yellow and black+blue.
- The yellow and blue are for highlighting key words.
- Black+blue is for when the headline is on light backgrounds and white+yellow is on dark backgrounds.
- Supporting text should be white on dark backgrounds and black on light backgrounds.

## 05 No ellipsis in headlines (picture: 05-no-ellipsis.png, "UNCOMMON SCHOOLS ARE..." shown as the example not to follow)
- We do not use ellipsis in headlines.

## 06 Regional cards (pictures: 06a three columns with Find a School as sixth card, 06b two columns, 06c Find a School card alone, 06d circle arrow CTA)
- Regional cards should be displayed in either columns of 3 or 2.
- The "Find a School" card should be paired with the regional card grouping.
- The regional cards always use the circle secondary CTA.

## 07 Dark colour is black (picture: 07-black-not-navy.png, the black Why Families section)
- We use black whenever a dark colour is necessary.
- Never dark navy.

## 08 Secondary colours (picture: 08-secondary-colours.webp, the media wall with the pale blue "Joyful" and peach "Rigorous" tiles)
- Where possible, use secondary colours from the brand guidelines to add a touch of variation. Never in a significant way.

## 09 Photography instead of generic icons (picture: 09-photos-not-icons.webp, the Alumni / Educator Resources / Careers cards)
- We should eliminate the use of generic icons in place of photography and colourful accent shapes and designs.
- The icons feel generic and do not add any emotion or connection to a card.

## 10 Footer (picture: 10-footer.webp)
- This is the footer of choice.
- The four buttons are white stroke outlines and never filled or coloured.
- The column orientation is our choice from the variations provided.

## 11 Announcement bar (picture: 11-announcement-bar-black.webp, sent 2026-11-12 after Courtney's comment)
- The announcement bar is black with white text, the highlighted words in yellow, white and yellow circle arrows. Find a School stays the only yellow block at the top of the page.
